Privacy policy

Heedsy doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

What you type, your favorites, your recent messages and your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, your favorites sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. We can't see them.

Microphone and speech recognition

Listening mode uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to show what's said on the screen. Apple handles the audio, on your device or on Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy. Heedsy doesn't keep or send it anywhere else. Listening only starts when you tap the microphone.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the voices built into iOS, on your device.

Widgets and Shortcuts

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts only read and change the message on your own device.
