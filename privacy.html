Privacy policy

Heedsy has no accounts and no ads, and it doesn't track you. It does count which features get used, without what you write, and you can turn that off.

What stays on your device

What you type, your saved notes, your recent messages and your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, your saved notes sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. We can't see them.

Usage statistics

Heedsy tells Amplitude, an analytics service, when things happen in the app: which screen opened, which button or effect was tapped, where a message came from (typed, spoken or saved) and roughly how long it was. It never sends what you write or say. Each phone gets a random ID made up by Amplitude. No name, email, advertising ID, IP address or city goes with it, though Amplitude can tell the country from the connection. To stop it, turn off Share usage statistics in Settings → Preferences.

Microphone and speech recognition

Listening mode uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to show what's said on the screen. Apple handles the audio, on your device or on Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy. Heedsy doesn't keep or send it anywhere else. Listening only starts when you tap the microphone.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the voices built into iOS, on your device.

Widgets and Shortcuts

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts only read and change the message on your own device.
