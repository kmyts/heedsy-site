Heedsy

Heedsy shows your message on the whole screen, as big as it fits. Just type the text and Heedsy will do the rest.

How it works

  • Tap anywhere to type, tap again to show.
  • Shake the phone or long-press the text to make the screen flash.
  • Tap the heart to save a message. Saved messages show up above the keyboard.
  • Tap the microphone and Heedsy shows what's said in big letters.
  • Pick a style, colors and a font in Settings.
